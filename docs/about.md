# About the Atlas

OM Firmware Atlas connects independent Olympus and OM SYSTEM firmware work. It grew from the Caduceus community and the earlier OpenM43 link catalogue, with a broader remit than any one mod family.

The repository holds documentation, structured research records and the scripts that validate and publish them. It does not host vendor firmware, firmware builders, emulators or a new look editor. [Upstream projects](../views/projects/index.md) retain their implementation, support and licensing decisions.

The initial material is a bounded synthesis of public sources examined on 17 November 2026. It is neither an exhaustive camera inventory nor an independent reproduction of upstream tests. Evidence pages expose their provenance and missing details. [Read the evidence model](evidence.md).

Original Atlas contributions use the repository's [Apache-2.0 licence](../LICENSE). Third-party works remain under their own terms. No affiliation with Olympus or OM Digital Solutions is implied.

## Disclaimer

The Atlas exists to point to other people's work. It links to independent repositories, releases, tools and discussions so that they are easier to find, but it does not host them, and it holds no responsibility for their contents. That includes any firmware, builders, executables or other downloads they offer, and their accuracy, licensing, safety and availability.

An entry summarises what its source says, with the date it was checked. A listing is not an endorsement, a review of the project's code or files, or a promise that anything is correct, current or safe to use. Sources can change after they are checked.

Modifying camera firmware can stop a camera from working, void its warranty and lose photos or settings. Anything you download, build or install from a linked project is at your own risk and under that project's own terms. Ask the project's maintainers for support, not the Atlas.

If you think a listed source should not be here, or an entry misrepresents it, [open an issue](https://github.com/xulfr/om-firmware-atlas/issues/new).
